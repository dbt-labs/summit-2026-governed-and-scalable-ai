Explain the business domains and source systems, dbt project structure, staging/intermediate/mart responsibilities, important macros, tests and contracts, governed semantic assets, and which parts of the project are complete versus intentionally left for the workshop.

Ground the walkthrough in active project-owned files and give me a short recommended reading path. Do not modify files or run warehouse writes. Skip `models/answer_key/`, `training_assets/`, `models/warlock/`, `models/wizard/`, `target/`, `logs/`, and `dbt_packages/`.
